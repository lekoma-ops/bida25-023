Our Story • Since 2010

GoHard Clothing BW was born in the streets of Francistown in 2010. What started as a small passion project by local creatives has grown into Botswana’s loudest voice in streetwear.

We don’t just make clothes — we create culture. From acid-wash drops that lit up Gaborone to windbreakers that survive the Botswana heat and dust, every piece is designed for the ones who GO HARD every single day.

Physical stores in Francistown (Barclays Plaza) and Gaborone (Mainmall Old CBD). Worn by artists, athletes, and everyday hustlers across Southern Africa.
